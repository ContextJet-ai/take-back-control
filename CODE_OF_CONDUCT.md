# Code of Conduct

This project helps people in distress. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to act with care.

## Our pledge

We want participation to be harassment-free and respectful for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, religion or orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and specific. Assume good faith.
- Accept corrections gracefully. Much of this project is people fixing each other's mistakes.
- Centre the people who will use the site.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- **Sharing real intimate images, or real victims' names, links or case details, anywhere in this project.** Remove them immediately if you see them, and tell a maintainer.
- Using the project to locate, expose or pressure any person.
- Spam, or promoting a service that charges victims for removal.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn or block participants who break these rules. To report a problem, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** if it is urgent or sensitive, or open an issue if it is not.
